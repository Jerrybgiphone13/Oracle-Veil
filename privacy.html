Oracle Veil

Privacy

Oracle Veil is designed to work without accounts, advertising trackers, or payment information.

Information on your device

Your current ritual, settings, and question may be stored in your browser so you can continue after reloading. You can remove this information by clearing this site’s browser data.

Personal interpretations

If you request a generated personal interpretation, your question and selected card names are sent to the Oracle Veil server and then to Google’s Gemini service to create the response. Oracle Veil does not intentionally store that request or response on its server. Hosting and service providers may process ordinary technical logs under their own policies.

Optional external services

Background music is loaded from Wikimedia only after you enable it. Sharing controls may open the selected social platform. Oracle Veil does not receive your account credentials from those services.
